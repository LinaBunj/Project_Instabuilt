Construction System

A single, precision-engineered platform — hybrid steel-and-timber modules, digitally designed and factory manufactured — behind every InstaBuilt product line.

Hybrid steel & timber Factory precision Up to 75% faster
The system

One platform, every product.

From a 28 m² POP UP to a multistory building, everything shares the same module logic — engineered digitally, manufactured to specification, and assembled on site in a fraction of the time.

  • Hybrid steel-and-timber module frame
  • Digital twin from first sketch to final bolt
  • Factory QA before a module leaves the line
  • Relocatable and reusable by design

How it works

From model to module in four steps.

01

Design & engineer

Your brief becomes a fully coordinated digital model — structure, services and finishes resolved before manufacture begins.

02

Manufacture

Modules are built on a production line under factory conditions, with quality checks at every station.

03

Deliver

Finished modules travel by road, protected and sequenced for just-in-time arrival on site.

04

Assemble & finish

Craned into place and connected in days; facade joints and services sealed to specification.
