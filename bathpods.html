The concept

The bathroom, perfected in the factory.

Bathrooms are the highest-risk room on any site. By building them under factory conditions, we eliminate leaks, defects and rework — and deliver a finished bathroom in a single crane lift.

  • Factory-tested plumbing and electrics
  • Waterproof, tile-ready or fully tiled
  • Accessible and standard layouts
  • Fits POP UP through to Signature Homes
