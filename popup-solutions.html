Product line

POP UP Solutions

Rapidly deployable, single-storey modular units for housing, hospitality, offices and emergency response — manufactured offsite, delivered complete, and live within weeks.

Sizes · 28 / 52 / 104 m² Deployment · weeks, not months KfW40 ready
The concept

A complete building, delivered to your site.

POP UP units are fully finished in the factory — structure, envelope, services, kitchen and bathroom included — then transported to site and connected in days.

  • Turnkey delivery — furniture and services included
  • Relocatable and reusable, with minimal groundworks
  • Foundation options from screw piles to strip footings
  • Customisable cladding, glazing and interior layout
