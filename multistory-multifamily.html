Multistory Multifamily

Prefabricated mid- and high-rise residential buildings that stack modules floor by floor — bringing the speed and quality of offsite construction to urban densification.

Up to 8+ storeys Modular core & envelope KfW40
The concept

Density, without the disruption.

Complete apartments arrive on site as finished modules and are craned into place storey by storey — dramatically cutting on-site time, noise and traffic for the surrounding neighbourhood.

  • Stacked modular construction with hybrid steel core
  • Facade systems tailored to the local context
  • Reduced on-site noise, dust and deliveries
  • Fixed programme and cost, agreed up front
