Why InstaBuilt

Three principles behind every build.

01 — Speed

Up to 75% faster

Modules are manufactured in the factory in parallel with site works, compressing programmes from years to months — and cutting disruption on site to days.

02 — Sustainability

KfW40 standard

Every building is engineered to KfW40 energy performance and beyond. Offsite production reduces waste, transport and embodied carbon at every stage.

03 — Precision

Engineering you can trust

Factory-controlled manufacturing means millimetre tolerances, predictable costs and consistent quality — verified before a single module leaves the line.

The construction system

One platform. Endless possibilities.

Our hybrid steel-and-timber modular system is the engine behind every product line. Units are designed digitally, manufactured to specification, and delivered complete with services and finishes.

  • Factory-controlled quality assurance on every unit
  • Parallel factory and site schedules
  • Transparent, fixed costs agreed up front

Sustainability

Built to KfW40. Designed for tomorrow.

Sustainability is not an add-on — it is engineered into the system. Low-embodied-carbon materials, energy-positive envelopes and circular construction, as standard.

  • KfW40 energy standard across the range
  • Low-embodied-carbon materials
  • Circular by design — modules can be relocated and reused
