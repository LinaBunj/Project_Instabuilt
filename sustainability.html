Sustainability

Sustainability

Sustainability is engineered into the system, not bolted on — from KfW40 energy performance to low-embodied-carbon materials and circular construction.

KfW40 standard Low embodied carbon Circular by design
Energy

KfW40 is our baseline.

Every InstaBuilt building meets the KfW40 efficiency standard — and can be upgraded toward KfW40+ and passive levels. Factory-built envelopes deliver airtightness and insulation that site work struggles to match.

  • KfW40 energy standard across the range
  • Triple glazing and high-performance insulation
  • Renewable heating and PV-ready design
  • Measured performance, not just modelled

Our commitments

Beyond the energy label.

Low embodied carbon

Timber-rich construction and responsibly sourced materials reduce the carbon locked into every building.

Circular by design

Modules can be disassembled, relocated and reused — extending a building's life beyond a single site.

Waste minimised

Offsite production reduces construction waste and site transport, with offcuts recycled at source.
