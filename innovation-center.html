Innovation Center

Innovation Center

Where we prototype the future of offsite — materials, systems and building technologies tested at full scale before they reach your project.

Materials testing Full-scale mock-ups Digital prototyping
The lab

A lab for the built environment.

Innovation in construction too often happens on the client's building. We've inverted that: new ideas are tested, measured and refined in our facilities — so only proven systems make it to your site.

  • Material and connection testing
  • Full-scale 1:1 module mock-ups
  • Digital prototyping and simulation
  • Research partnerships with universities

Focus areas

What we're working on.

Digital manufacturing

Digital twins, CNC-driven production and automated QA for repeatable, documented precision.

Sustainable materials

Low-carbon composites, bio-based insulation and circular components under continuous evaluation.

Systems integration

Smart-home, energy and assistive technologies pre-integrated in the factory, not retrofitted on site.

Prototyping

Full-scale, before full build.

Every system is prototyped at 1:1 before it reaches a client site. Detailing, tolerances and installation sequences are rehearsed in the factory — so risk is retired in our lab, not on your project.

  • Rehearsed installation sequences
  • Detail and tolerance verification
  • Client walk-throughs before production
